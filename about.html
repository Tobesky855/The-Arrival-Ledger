About The Arrival Ledger

Financial systems are usually organized by product, institution and country. People's financial lives rarely are.

The Arrival Ledger is an independent research platform examining financial lives across borders.

Our work investigates how immigrant and newcomer households interact with U.S.fiancial systems, how diaspora households build and move wealth across countries, and how institutions respond to financial lives that do not fit neatly within one market.

We combine secondary research and financial-services analysis with an emerging program of original household and practitioner research.

Our goal is to produce evidence that helps people and institutions better understand financial life across borders.
