Financial life across borders

Financial systems are often organized by product, institution and country. People's financial lives rarely are. The Arrival Ledger studies what happens when financial lives extend across those boundaries.

One financial life. More than one financial system.

Some households earn, save, borrow, invest, support family and own assets across more than one country. The Arrival Ledger examines how these interconnected financial decisions shape financial health, wealth and economic mobility.
