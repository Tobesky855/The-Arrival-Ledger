Research

Evidence for understanding financial lives across borders.

U.S. Immigrant & Newcomer Financial Systems

We examine how immigrants and newcomers establish financial lives in the United States—from building credit and accessing banking and housing to navigating systems that may not recognize financial histories, assets or experience established elsewhere.

Core research question

How can U.S. financial institutions better understand and serve immigrant and newcomer households while maintaining sound risk and business decisions?

Areas of inquiry

  • Financial identity and recognition
  • Credit access and decision systems
  • Banking and financial health
  • Housing and economic mobility
  • Product and institutional design

Diaspora & Cross-Border Capital

We examine how African diaspora households move from sending money and supporting family across borders toward investment, ownership and long-term wealth creation—and the financial systems that enable or constrain that transition.

Core research question

What enables diaspora capital to move beyond remittances toward investment and ownership, and what prevents households, institutions and credible opportunities from connecting?

Areas of inquiry

  • Remittances and family financial obligations
  • Diaspora investment and ownership
  • Trust and investor confidence
  • Cross-border wealth building
  • Financial infrastructure and capital formation
